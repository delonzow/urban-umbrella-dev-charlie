How to Play

Goal

Eat pears and survive five levels.

Controls

Move your mouse around the game board. The worm follows your cursor.

You can also use WASD or the arrow keys.

Press Space to pause.

Items

  • 🍐 Pear = +10 points
  • ✨ Golden Pear = +30 points
  • 💥 Hazard = lose 1 HP
  • 🛡️ Shield = block one hit
  • 💚 Health = restore HP
